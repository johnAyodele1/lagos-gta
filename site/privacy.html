Data handled by this prototype

The current prototype does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. It is designed not to send gameplay personal data to a game server.

Local storage

The game stores the version of the Terms & Conditions accepted and an acceptance timestamp in the device's application storage. This is used only to remember that the current legal notice was accepted. The prototype does not upload this record.

Data not currently collected by game features

The current prototype does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or payment details.

Third-party services and future changes

No online multiplayer, cloud save, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or payment service is currently integrated. This policy must be updated before any such service or SDK is added. The publisher must inventory the data processed by the game engine, all SDKs, platform integrations and backend services.

Retention and deletion

The local consent record remains on the device until app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. The prototype has no remote player account or game-server profile to delete. If account creation is introduced, account and associated-data deletion must be implemented before release in line with applicable store policies and law.

Children and age rating

The target audience and final age rating have not yet been set. Store declarations must accurately describe the released game's content and data practices.

Contact

The legal publisher name and monitored privacy contact address must be completed before public release.
