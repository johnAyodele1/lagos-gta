# Lagos GTA — Privacy Policy

**Status: development draft — publisher/contact details and public hosting required before store submission.**  
**Version:** 1  
**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

## Who publishes this game?

The legal publisher identity and privacy contact address must be completed before public release. They are not fabricated in this draft.

## Data collected by the current prototype

The current prototype:
- does not require account registration or login;
- does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or payment details;
- does not include advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs;
- does not currently send gameplay personal data to a game backend.

The game stores a local legal-consent version and acceptance timestamp in the device's application storage. This record is used to remember that the player accepted the current in-game terms. It is not uploaded by the current prototype.

## Network and third-party services

No online multiplayer, cloud-save, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or payment service is currently integrated. This policy must be revised before adding any such SDK or service. The developer must inventory the data collected by the game engine, every SDK, platform integration and backend, and disclose the purpose, lawful basis where applicable, recipients, transfers and retention.

## Data retention and deletion

The local legal-consent record remains on the device until the player clears the app's storage or uninstalls the game. The current prototype has no remote player account or game-server profile to delete.

If accounts or server-side data are added, the game must implement the account/data deletion processes required by the applicable stores and laws before enabling account creation.

## Children's privacy and age rating

The final target audience and age rating have not yet been set. The public release must complete accurate age-rating and target-audience declarations and comply with applicable child-safety and family policies.

## Your choices and contact

The in-game LEGAL screen provides access to this notice. A public privacy contact and a publicly accessible, non-geofenced privacy-policy URL must be supplied before store submission.

## Changes to this policy

This policy must be updated whenever data practices change. The store privacy declarations must match the actual app, SDK and backend behaviour.

---

This is a factual policy draft for the current development prototype, not legal advice. Do not submit it as a final policy until the publisher identity, contact, hosting URL, data inventory and legal review are complete.
